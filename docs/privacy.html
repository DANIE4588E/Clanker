Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

Clanker is a chat app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: Clanker has no servers and no accounts. Your chats, files and sign-ins are stored on your phone, and the app sends data only to the AI provider and the services you choose to connect.

What stays on your phone

  • Chats, files and settings are stored in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • API keys and sign-in tokens for AI providers and connected services are stored in your device’s secure keychain.
  • We do not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers or any other usage data, and we do not run servers that receive your data.

Where data goes

Your AI provider

To answer you, Clanker sends your messages, and anything an agent reads while working on your request, to the AI provider or endpoint you set up in the app (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or a server you run). That provider’s own terms and privacy policy apply to what it receives. If you use an on-device model, this data never leaves your phone.

Services you connect (plugins)

When you add a plugin, you sign in on the service’s own page and approve the access it asks for. Clanker then talks to that service directly from your phone. An agent uses a plugin only while working on a request you made in a chat. What it reads from the service may be shown to your AI provider as part of that chat, and files it downloads are saved in the chat’s private workspace on your phone. You can make any plugin read-only, or remove it, under Plugins in the app.

Other requests the app makes

  • Web searches and pages an agent opens go to the search provider or website involved.
  • The app checks GitHub for new versions. This request contains no personal data.
  • If you pair a remote agent backend that you run yourself, chats in it are sent to that device.

Google user data

If you connect a Google plugin, Clanker asks Google only for the access that plugin needs, and only when you add it. Clanker uses this access only to do what you ask in your chats:

Plugin Access Used to
Gmail Read, compose, send and organise mail Search and summarise your mail, draft and send replies you ask for, and label, archive or trash messages you choose.
Google Calendar See and edit events Show your schedule, find free time, and create, change or cancel events you ask for.
Google Drive See, edit, create and delete files Find files, bring them into a chat, and save or organise files you ask for.
Google Docs, Sheets, Slides See and edit documents, spreadsheets and presentations Read them and make the edits or new files you ask for.
Google Tasks See and edit tasks Show, add, update and complete tasks you ask about.
All Google plugins Your name and email address Show which Google account is connected.

Google user data is fetched directly from Google to your phone. It is stored only on your phone (for example in your chats and the chat’s workspace) and shared only with the AI provider you chose, as part of the chat in which you asked for it, to provide that feature. Clanker does not sell Google user data, does not use or transfer it for advertising, does not use it to train or improve AI or machine-learning models, and does not let any person read it. Because Clanker has no servers, nobody but you and the providers you chose ever receives it.

Clanker’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting a chat deletes its messages and its workspace files.
  • Removing a plugin, or disconnecting its account in the plugin’s settings, deletes the sign-in from your phone and, where the service allows it, revokes Clanker’s access.
  • You can also remove Clanker’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.

Security

Sign-ins use each service’s own page with OAuth and PKCE, tokens are kept in the device keychain, and all connections to providers and services use HTTPS.

Children

Clanker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
