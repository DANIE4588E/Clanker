Your AI, your models, your apps.

Clanker is a chat app for Android and iOS that talks to the AI models you choose: your own API keys, your own server, or models running on the phone itself. Its agents can work with files, search the web, and, with your permission, work in apps like Gmail, Google Drive and Notion.

What it does

Bring your own models

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio, or a model on your phone.

Agents that do the work

Read and write files, run code, search the web, and make documents, spreadsheets and charts.

Plugins

Connect Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Outlook, Notion, GitHub and more, so agents can work there for you.

Private by design

No Clanker account and no Clanker servers. Your chats and sign-ins stay on your phone.

How plugins use your data

When you add a plugin, you sign in on that service’s own page and choose what to allow. Clanker keeps the sign-in on your phone and talks to the service directly. An agent reads or changes things there only while working on a request you made in a chat, and you can make any plugin read-only or remove it at any time. The details, including how Clanker handles Google user data, are in the privacy policy.
